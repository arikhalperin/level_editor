## Risks and skipped work

- The wall-standoff test asserts only a lower bound on the gap (>= 44 px); a probe distance larger than 50 would still pass.
- Both orc-stop tests finish with only ~2-3 px of margin because the orc creeps ~1.7 px on each re-charge cycle; a small timing change could flip them.
- the_club_misses_outside_its_separation_box does not assert the club actually went live during the loop (it does in fact), so it could become vacuous if the window logic regressed.
- The club's 82 px Y separation bound has no negative test; only the X bound is exercised behaviourally.
- an_attack_press_is_buffered_until_the_cooldown_clears exercises only a 0.03 s gap of the 0.12 s buffer, because SLASH_COOLDOWN - SLASH_FORWARD_DURATION is 0.03.
- The 0 s wind-up out of a sword freeze, the min-moving-speed give-up, and refusal to swing while climbing are inspection-only.
- Overlay text, F2 drawing, key mapping and non-destructiveness have no automated coverage; main.rs contains no tests.
- The orc's fall uses base GRAVITY only, omitting PLAYER_FREE_FALL_EXTRA_GRAVITY — true to the game's plain-gravity orc but not to the player's exact fall curve.
- The orc body is the fixed 38/32 capsule and ignores the level entity's size, so the spec's mapping row reads as a mismatch even though it is faithful to the game's setup_orc.
- blocked_ahead is a single point sample at 50 px ahead at the orc's centre Y, so a thin wall, or one whose top is below the orc's centre, is not detected; ground_ahead samples every 4 px.
- Level bitmap textures keep drawing during play, so a defeated orc's or collected coin's artwork remains on screen behind the simulated shapes.
- Archived specs are currently contradicted and have not been edited: play-simulation/spec.md:21 says bitmap behaviour is out of scope, :46 omits J/X and L, :58 describes the pre-combat overlay; editor-help-screen/spec.md:23-39 claims to list every key while omitting J/X, L and F2.
